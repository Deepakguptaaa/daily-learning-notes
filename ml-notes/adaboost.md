# AdaBoost

## What is AdaBoost?

AdaBoost (Adaptive Boosting) is an ensemble learning algorithm that combines multiple weak learners to create a stronger model.

Most commonly, Decision Trees with one split (decision stumps) are used as weak learners.

## How AdaBoost Works

1. Train a weak learner.
2. Identify incorrectly classified samples.
3. Increase the weight of those samples.
4. Train the next learner.
5. Combine all learners to make the final prediction.

## Advantages

* High accuracy
* Reduces bias
* Works well for classification tasks

## Limitations

* Sensitive to noisy data
* Can be slower than simple models

## Applications

* Face detection
* Fraud detection
* Customer classification
* Medical diagnosis
