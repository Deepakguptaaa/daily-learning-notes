# Ensemble Learning

## What is Ensemble Learning?

Ensemble Learning is a machine learning technique that combines multiple models to produce better predictions than a single model.

The main idea is that a group of models often performs better than one individual model.

## Why Use Ensemble Learning?

* Improves prediction accuracy
* Reduces overfitting
* Increases model stability
* Produces more reliable results

## Types of Ensemble Learning

### Bagging

Bagging trains multiple models independently on different subsets of the training data.

Example:

* Random Forest

### Boosting

Boosting trains models sequentially.

Each new model focuses on correcting the mistakes made by previous models.

Examples:

* AdaBoost
* Gradient Boosting
* XGBoost

### Stacking

Stacking combines predictions from multiple different models and uses another model to make the final prediction.

## Advantages

* Higher accuracy
* Better generalization
* More robust predictions

## Applications

* Fraud detection
* Medical diagnosis
* Credit scoring
* Recommendation systems

## What I Learned

* Ensemble Learning combines multiple models.
* Bagging, Boosting, and Stacking are the main ensemble techniques.
* Ensemble methods often outperform individual models.
